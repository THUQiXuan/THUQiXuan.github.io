I am a final-year undergraduate student at the Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, Yao Class) at Tsinghua University, directed by Andrew C. Yao (Turing Award laureate 2000).

My research broadly focuses on AI agents and LLM alignment. I am particularly interested in:

  • Self-evolving agents that dynamically acquire and adapt tools/skills (MCP-based frameworks)
  • Data-centric alignment: how supervision signals transfer across modalities and tasks, and how to select/weight training data efficiently
  • Agentic systems for research: automated research idea generation and validation

Internships & Research:

  • Xiaohongshu Hi Lab (Mar 2026–present): AI agents (ACE Top Intern)
  • University of Pennsylvania, Cognitive Computation Group (May–Aug 2025): multimodal reasoning (Advisor: Prof. Dan Roth)
  • Princeton University, Wang Lab (Nov 2024–Feb 2026): Co-led Alita and Alita-G (Advisor: Prof. Mengdi Wang)
