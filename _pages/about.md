I am a final-year undergraduate student at the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences (IIIS, Yao Class)](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) at Tsinghua University, directed by [Andrew C. Yao](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/yao/) (Turing Award laureate 2000).

My research broadly focuses on **AI agents** and **LLM alignment**. I am particularly interested in:
- **Self-evolving agents** that dynamically acquire and adapt tools/skills (MCP-based frameworks)
- **Data-centric alignment**: how supervision signals transfer across modalities and tasks, and how to select/weight training data efficiently
- **Agentic systems for research**: automated research idea generation and validation
