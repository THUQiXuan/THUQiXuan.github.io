Our paper **"Demystifying Supervision Data Generalization in Multimodal LMs"** has been accepted at **ICLR 2026**!

We study how supervision signals transfer across modalities and tasks, and use these findings to perform real-data reweighting and synthetic-data selection. Our method significantly outperforms strong baselines under the same training budget.
